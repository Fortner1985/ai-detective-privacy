A.I. Detective apps — Privacy Policy

Effective 27 September 2026 · Apps: A.I. Video Detective (com.fortner.aidetective) and A.I. Image Detective (com.fortner.aiimagedetective) · Developer: John R Fortner

In short: This policy covers both apps. Neither app collects, sends or shares any personal data. Every check runs on your phone. Neither app has internet permission, so nothing you scan can leave your device through it.

What the app does

A.I. Video Detective looks at a video you choose, and A.I. Image Detective looks at a photo you choose. Each reports forensic indicators of how the file may have been made or changed. All analysis happens on your device, in the app you opened.

Information collected

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it declares no internet permission.

Permissions

Neither app requests any Android permission. You give the app access to one photo or video at a time, by choosing it in the system picker or by sharing it to the app. That access covers only the item you chose.

What stays on your device

  • Scan history. For each scan the app keeps a short record on your phone: the date, the file's name, its type and the result shown to you. It does not keep a copy of the photo or video, a thumbnail, the file's location or any image made during the scan.
  • One setting about your device's processing level.

Marks you tap on a picture (shadows, reflections, eyes, outlines) are kept in the app's memory only while that scan's report is open. They are never written to storage and are not part of the history.

You can clear the history at any time on the History screen, and uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. The app's data is excluded from Android cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer, so it does not leave the phone that way either.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
